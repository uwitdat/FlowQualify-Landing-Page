Three things to have ready

The more you bring, the deeper it goes.

Not required. But each of these lets Phil skip the surface read and go straight to the dynamics in your specific account.

01
Your last 90 days of ROAS broken out by campaign, not blended

A blended number hides where efficiency is actually coming from. Campaign-level data tells Phil which structures are carrying the account and which are diluting it. Pull this from Google Ads or Meta Business Suite before the call.

02
A rough sense of your margin profile by product category

ROAS targets only mean something relative to actual margins. Knowing which products carry the most profit changes where budget should go, what to push in Shopping, and what your real efficiency floor is. It does not need to be exact.

03
Read access to your Google Ads or Meta account if possible

Phil reviews your account before the call. If you can add him as a read-only user in advance, the findings go deeper. If not, a screenshot of your campaign view works fine. Either way, he comes prepared.
