Before You Apply

The questions every serious founder asks before booking.

Is the Growth Diagnostic actually free?
+
Yes. No charge, no obligation, no retainer required. We do this because the fastest way to show you what we find is to find it.
What makes this different from a sales call?
+
It's a two-part diagnostic, not a pitch. First we learn your business on a short call. Then we audit your actual accounts off-call and build a 30-point recommendation deck specific to your situation — delivered as a PDF on a second call. You receive the full deck regardless of whether we work together. Most agencies save that kind of analysis for paying clients.
We've hired agencies before and it didn't work out. Why would this be different?
+
That's exactly the kind of experience we hear about. Most of the time, the agency was working inside a single channel while the constraint lived somewhere else — in CRM, in creative, in attribution, in audience architecture. The diagnostic maps all of it before we touch anything.
Do you work with brands at our stage ($500K–$2M in revenue)?
+
Yes. Our diagnostic is designed for established brands — not startups. If you're generating $500K+ per year and growth has plateaued or scaled inefficiently, this is the right conversation.
What platforms do you cover?
+
Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Ads (Search, Shopping, YouTube) are the core platforms. We also run active accounts on Microsoft/Bing Ads, TikTok Ads, and Pinterest Ads. Within any platform we audit, we assess creative strategy, remarketing, tracking, campaign structure, and bidding. If you're only running one platform, we go deeper on that one.
How long does the diagnostic take?
+
The first discovery call is short — typically 20–30 minutes. We do the account review and audit off-call on our own time. The second call, where we walk you through your 30-point PDF, usually runs 30–45 minutes.
What happens after the diagnostic?
+
We walk you through your 30-point recommendation deck on a second call, answer any questions, and leave you with the PDF. If it looks like we'd be a strong fit to implement together, we'll discuss what that looks like. No pressure, no hard sell.
How many diagnostics do you take on?
+
We limit availability each month to ensure every diagnostic gets the depth and attention it requires. We’ll let you know during the introductory call if capacity is currently open.

Our agency relationship with Google currently includes early access to select features not yet in general availability.
