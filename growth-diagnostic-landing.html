01 Attribution High Priority
1.

Meta and Google are double-claiming 38% of your conversions. $31,200 in monthly conversion credit is assigned to both platforms simultaneously. You are scaling Meta spend on performance data that includes revenue Google already owns.

Action Implement a unified attribution model with a 1-day click, 0-day view window. Reallocate $12,000/mo from branded search — which is attributing organic traffic — into Meta prospecting.
2.

Branded spend accounts for 41% of your reported Google revenue. This traffic was converting before the ad ran. You are paying to claim demand that was already yours.

Action Segment branded from non-branded in reporting. Reduce branded budget to a defensive floor of $800/mo and reallocate the remainder to non-branded prospecting.
02 Campaign Structure High Priority
1.

6 of 7 active campaigns are below Google's 30-conversion learning threshold, averaging 127 monthly conversions split across 7 campaigns. Smart Bidding has no statistical basis to set competitive bids; every campaign is in permanent learning mode.

Action Consolidate to 3 campaigns by performance tier. Concentrate budget into the top tier to clear the learning threshold within 6–8 weeks and unlock Target ROAS bidding at scale.

This diagnostic is not for everyone.

Request access if

Before You Apply

The questions every serious founder asks before booking.

Is the Growth Diagnostic actually free?
+
Yes. No charge, no obligation, no retainer required. We do this because the fastest way to show you what we find is to find it.
What makes this different from a sales call?
+
It's a diagnostic, not a pitch. We spend 15 minutes understanding your business, then 3 to 5 days inside your actual accounts building a 30-point report specific to your situation. Granting read-only access before the call is what makes the findings targeted to you rather than generic. You receive the full report on the review call regardless of whether you work with us. Most agencies reserve that level of analysis for paying clients.
We've hired agencies before and it didn't work out. Why would this be different?
+
That's exactly the kind of experience we hear about. Most of the time, the agency was optimizing inside one channel while the real constraint lived somewhere else: attribution, creative, audience architecture, or tracking. The diagnostic maps all of it before we touch anything.
Do you work with brands at our stage ($500K–$2M in revenue)?
+
Yes. The diagnostic is built for established ecommerce brands generating $500K or more per year. If you're spending on paid media and growth has plateaued or returns have started to slide, this is worth your time.
What if my brand is doing less than $500K per year?
+
The diagnostic is designed for brands with enough ad data to make the findings meaningful, typically $500K or more in annual revenue and at least $10K per month in active paid media. If you're approaching that range and running campaigns on Meta or Google, apply anyway and we'll take a look. We occasionally work with brands earlier in their growth when the ad activity is there and the fit is strong.
What platforms do you cover?
+
Meta (Facebook/Instagram) and Google Ads (Search, Shopping, YouTube) are the core platforms. We also run active accounts on Microsoft/Bing Ads, TikTok Ads, and Pinterest Ads. Within any platform we audit, we assess creative strategy, remarketing, tracking, campaign structure, and bidding. If you're only running one platform, we go deeper on that one.
How long does the diagnostic take?
+
The kickoff call is 15 minutes. We then spend 3 to 5 days in your accounts on our own time. The review call, where we walk you through your 30-point report, runs about 30 minutes. Under an hour of your time in total.
What happens after the diagnostic?
+
We walk you through every finding on the review call, answer any questions, and send the full PDF for you to keep. If it looks like a strong fit to work together, we'll outline what that looks like. If not, the report is yours to act on however you choose.
How many diagnostics do you take on?
+
We limit the number of diagnostics we run each month so each one gets the attention it needs. Applications are reviewed before a call is booked, so you'll know if a spot is available before you schedule anything.
Can I speak with a past client before deciding?
+
Yes. We work under NDAs so client names stay private, but we can arrange a reference call. Just mention it when you apply and we'll set it up.
Do I need to share access to my ad accounts?
+
Yes. Read-only access is what makes the findings specific to your accounts rather than generic. We'll send instructions after your kickoff call. You stay in full control: you can revoke access at any time, and we never make changes without being formally engaged. If you're not comfortable sharing read-only access, the diagnostic isn't something we can do well.
What does it cost to work with you after the diagnostic?
+
We don't publish rates because every engagement is scoped to the brand. Ad spend volume, platforms, and what we find in the diagnostic all factor in. What we can tell you: you'll get a specific number on the review call, not a range. No "starting from" pricing, no vague proposals. If the fit isn't there financially, we'll say so.

Experience Creates
Earlier Visibility.

Our team's experience managing significant advertising spend and working closely with major advertising platforms gives us visibility into select Google and Meta alpha and beta opportunities, emerging capabilities that many advertisers may not encounter until broader release.

For eligible accounts, this can create opportunities to evaluate new advertising capabilities before they become widely available. But early access alone is not an advantage. The value comes from knowing what is worth testing, how to test it responsibly, and whether it can contribute to profitable growth.

Early Experience in Action

Our team gained early experience with Google Performance Max during its beta period, developing a grounded understanding of how product feeds, conversion tracking, audience signals, creative assets, and first-party customer data influence automated ecommerce performance, before most advertisers had meaningful exposure to the format.

Current Alpha & Beta Access

Our agency relationship with Google currently includes early access to select features not yet in general availability. Active programs include PMax Channel Reporting (campaign-level channel attribution within Performance Max), AI Max for Search (intent-expansion matching beyond traditional keywords), Smart Bidding Exploration (which surfaces 18–19% more unique conversion opportunities by expanding bidding to lower-competition queries), and gender-based audience exclusions within PMax. These are evaluated on a per-account basis — not applied by default — and only deployed where the data supports it.

We don't chase new features because they're new. We evaluate selectively, measure against clear business outcomes, and scale only when the data supports it.

Availability varies by platform, market, account eligibility, and individual program requirements.
